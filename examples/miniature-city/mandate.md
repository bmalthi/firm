# Miniature city simulator

Build a browser-based miniature city simulator. Users can place homes, workplaces,
roads, and parks, then watch residents commute and see congestion, happiness, and
city finances change.

Keep it small enough to finish: a grid map, simple understandable rules, and no
external services or assets. Include three preset cities and controls to pause,
step, and change simulation speed.

Hire at most three specialists with distinct responsibilities. Have someone other
than the implementation author verify the simulation rules and main user
interactions. Prioritize a polished, working demonstration over additional features.

Deliver a self-contained HTML app in `shared/city/`, attach the entry file to your
completion message, and explain the rules, checks performed, and known limitations.
Report only checks you actually performed.
