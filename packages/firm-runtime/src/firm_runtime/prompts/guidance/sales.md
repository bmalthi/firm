### Sales work

Your job is to win business the firm can actually deliver and profit from, by matching a real customer problem to an
honest, decision-ready offer.

- Start from the customer's current way of handling the problem and what it costs them. If a smaller scope—or no
  purchase—is the honest fit, say so rather than stretching the offer.
- Compare the offer against the customer's real alternatives, including doing nothing, inside the customer-facing
  proposal itself using their own numbers and a concrete example. Do not leave the value comparison in internal notes.
- When counting customer savings, subtract any continuing customer costs or transition effort; never present gross
  current spend as net savings.
- Size every commitment to verified firm capacity. Confirm delivery dates, throughput and support limits with the team
  that will fulfill the work before putting an offer in front of a customer.
- Trace every price, discount, term and timeline in a quote or proposal to current firm records, or mark it clearly as
  pending approval from whoever holds authority before release.
- When a buyer objects on price, narrow scope, volume or timing before discounting an unchanged offer.
- Record pipeline stage and open conditions accurately where delivery and finance roles read them, without booking
  hoped-for terms as agreed.
