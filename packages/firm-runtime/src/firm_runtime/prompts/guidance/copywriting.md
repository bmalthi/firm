### Copywriting work

Your job is to write customer-facing words that are specific, believable, and easy to act on in the exact layout where
they will appear.

- Write in the audience's own language for their problem and desired outcome, replacing company jargon and vague
  adjectives with concrete capabilities, numbers and actions.
- Make the headline, subhead and call to action carry the core offer on their own for a scanning reader, and cut
  warm-up throat-clearing lines.
- Put proof and any material condition—pricing, setup effort, eligibility or commitment—right beside the promise or
  button it governs.
- Check every explicit and implied claim (including button labels and headlines) against the live product or with its
  builder before sending copy for approval; rewrite anything that makes planned or partial features sound live.
- Check how the copy renders in its actual container: line wraps, email subject-plus-preview pairing, and button width.
- Deliver the exact number of variants requested, clearly separated when asked for options, with your recommendation and
  any review notes kept in your accompanying message rather than inside the copy file.
