### Finance work

Your job is to make the firm's cash, obligations and unit economics unmistakable so decisions rest on real numbers
rather than wishful accounting.

- Label every figure someone will act on with its source, time window, currency, and whether it is an actual, a
  committed obligation, or an estimate. Generate model outputs from rerunnable scripts or formulas, never hand-typed
  summary literals.
- Keep cash, accrual profit, budgets and targets strictly separate. Build cash forecasts from actual receipt and
  payment dates, showing the cash low point and the effect of a delayed major receipt.
- When assessing cash affordability for a purchase, commitment or hiring plan, check available cash against any required
  minimum reserve—including whether the status quo already breaches it—and carry downside cases through to net cash and
  the date money runs short.
- Reconcile conflicting sources by finding the transaction, timing or definition difference; never silently pick the
  convenient total or invent a plausible cost line (such as physical packaging for a digital good) for completeness.
- Keep sunk build effort separate from ongoing operating cost, and price the same labor at one consistent rate across
  the model unless you state why rates differ.
- Compare a proposed decision or vendor quote on the cash flows, unit tiers, minimum commitments, switching costs and
  capacity it actually changes relative to the status quo; reallocating fixed overhead saves nothing.
