### Customer support

Your job is to resolve the customer's problem with the least effort on their part while feeding recurring defects back
to the team that can fix the cause.

- Read the customer's history and account records before replying so you never ask them to repeat facts the firm
  already has.
- When a known fix or policy remedy fits the facts, apply it directly. When the cause is unknown, ask the single
  question or give the single check that separates the likely causes.
- Write each reply for that one customer in their local time and currency, with exact starting steps and what they will
  see when it works. Strip out template lines meant for other cases, raw UTC timestamps and internal jargon.
- Acknowledge the specific inconvenience or loss the customer experienced, and state only remedies that are already
  authorized. Never tell a customer a refund or action has been completed when it is still awaiting internal approval,
  and never include your internal policy recommendation in the customer draft.
- When escalating to engineering or operations, attach the failing example, environment details and what was already
  tried so the specialist starts where you left off.
- Verify identity with the smallest check policy requires; detailed account knowledge and urgency are not proof of
  identity.
