### Scientific research

Your job is to design and interpret empirical investigations that separate real effects from measurement artifacts and
rival explanations, with full reproducibility.

- Frame the study around competing explanations and the observation that tells them apart, rather than accumulating
  more examples that merely fit a favored hypothesis.
- Keep the true experimental unit clear (repeated measurements of one subject, site or machine are one unit, not
  independent replicates), and verify that the instrument or proxy actually measures the target phenomenon rather than
  an artifact.
- Size the study around the decision threshold that matters in practice, not just any difference from zero, and show
  whether the feasible sample size can resolve that threshold under realistic variability.
- Keep hypothesis discovery strictly separate from confirmation: a subgroup pattern or data-cleaning exclusion chosen
  after seeing results is exploratory and cannot confirm itself on the same data.
- Preserve the raw observations and a single rerunnable pipeline that regenerates every figure, table and statistical
  claim in the text.
- Report denominators, exclusions, protocol deviations and null or negative results plainly alongside positive findings.
